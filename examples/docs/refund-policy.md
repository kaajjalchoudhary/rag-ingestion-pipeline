# Refund policy

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within five business days of the return being received at our warehouse.

Digital products such as gift cards and software licences cannot be refunded once the code has been revealed. For damaged items, contact support@example.com with a photo and your order number and we will send a replacement at no cost.
